## Administrator and staff website

Sign in, then open [Admin Dashboard](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/admin). Its shortcuts are grouped by task; recently opened tools move toward the top. Admin actions also appear in the navbar bell. A count leaves the bell when the underlying action is resolved.

| What you want to do | Guide |
|---|---|
| Create/review court bookings and manage recurring court sessions | [Court operations](website/admin-courts.md) |
| Settle recurring court/lesson payments and award automatic points | [Recurring payments and points](website/recurring-payments-points.md) |
| Create lessons, enroll students, record attendance and settle fees | [Lesson Management](website/admin-lessons.md) |
| Arrange lessons at an external venue without a centre court | [Off-site lessons](website/offsite-lessons.md) |
| Review a student's account or reconcile historical records | [Student Accounts and audits](website/student-accounts.md) |
| Assign coaches and complete Foundation, Level and Skill Tree reviews | [Player Development](website/player-development.md) |
| Review/manual-create/correct invoices | [Admin invoices](website/admin-invoices.md) |
| Manage users, trials, assistant salary and manual reminders | [People and follow-up](website/people-follow-up.md) |
| Maintain products, orders, points, vouchers and promotions | [Commerce and rewards](website/commerce-rewards.md) |
| Connect the Codex assistant and manage its permissions | [MCP access and setup](mcp/getting-started.md) |

Coaches use their assigned-player workspace. Assistants use [My Salary](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/my-salary). These links appear in Account only when the role is active.

## Connected Codex assistant

MCP tools are for authorised administrators. Discovery and previews do not finalize changes. Audit drafts are saved working records; an audit changes the official account only after review and explicit approval. Other write workflows likewise require approval of the complete displayed preview.

| Ask Codex to… | Guide |
|---|---|
| Show overdue, upcoming and undated admin work | [Admin attention](mcp/admin-attention.md) |
| Reconcile historical lessons, payments and replacements | [Student audit](mcp/student-audit.md) |
| Award positive member points | [Point assignment](mcp/points.md) |
| Change specific lesson durations or prices | [Lesson changes](mcp/lesson-management.md) |
| Find missing attendance or record reviewed outcomes | [Attendance](mcp/attendance.md) |
| Prepare an already-paid manual invoice | [Manual invoices](mcp/manual-invoices.md) |

## Other pages and availability

- [Privacy Policy](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/privacy-policy), [Terms](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/terms), and [Data Deletion Instructions](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/data-deletion) explain the centre's published policies and request process.
- Older Bookings/Lessons admin links lead to the unified Schedule. Older Points and My Invoice Payments links lead to Leaderboard and My Payments. Shop category links lead to the corresponding shop tab.
- [Tier Preview](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/memberships/tier-preview) is an admin-only display of example tier cards, not a member statement.
- WhatsApp Cloud API ingestion/replies are an operator pilot with no inbox screen or MCP integration. The separate WhatsApp Reminders screen prepares messages for manual sending.
- Revenue reporting currently has no client dashboard. Background cleanup and renewal run automatically; clients do not manually trigger them here.

If a control is missing or a guide's screenshot differs, confirm your account role and the deployed version with the centre owner.
