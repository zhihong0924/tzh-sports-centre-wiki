## Released 16 October 2026

Available on the [live website](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/).

- **[30-minute court bookings](../website/30-minute-court-bookings.md):** Allowed when both adjacent slots are taken; otherwise minimum one hour. Separate selections each start with an hour and can be removed independently.
- **[Off-site lessons](../website/offsite-lessons.md):** Admins can create one-off or recurring lessons at an external location, including outside centre operating hours. Enrollment, attendance, fees, invoices and payment points follow the normal lesson workflow; no centre court is reserved.
- **[Off-site timetable](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/admin/lesson-fees):** The Off-site column appears after the centre courts, with equal-width lesson columns. Early/late sessions extend the timetable; overlapping external venues remain visible. Click a venue card to manage the lesson.
- **[Student lesson history](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/my-lessons):** Eligible absences stay visible with an Absent badge and replacement-token message. Students can check their entitlements in [My Tokens](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/my-tokens).
- **[Lesson payments and points](../website/offsite-lessons.md#check-attendance-student-history-and-payments):** Creating or renewing a lesson earns no points. Finalized payments earn one point per complete RM received; pending or rejected receipts earn none.
