# Reconcile a historical student account

[← MCP guides](README.md) · **Who:** Authorised administrators

**Try:**

> Use $tzh-student-account-audit to find Demo Member and prepare a draft audit named “2025 lessons” for 2025. Ask me to confirm the student. Do not commit anything.

1. Choose the matching student from discovery. For an existing case, give its readable name/date range; the assistant resolves its ID.
2. Supply historical lesson label, fee, duration and weekdays, with known per-date outcomes; or add an actually received payment, signed fee correction or replacement correction. The assistant asks for missing facts.
3. Upload evidence through the supported proof flow and say which entry/date it supports. Each image is limited to 3 MiB decoded. The assistant reports attachments, not an inspection of private image contents.
4. Ask **“Review the complete case; do not commit.”** Check every entry, proof count, warning/error, current balance, case effect, projected balance/replacements and validation version.
5. If correct, explicitly approve that exact reviewed version. Otherwise correct the draft and review again.

**Expected:** Draft entries save working data only. Commit applies the official history, received payments and applicable points. Historical payments use the case end date. Audit replacements are lifetime obligations, redeemed separately on the website. Draft entry removal and permanent draft/rejected case deletion need explicit confirmation; they cannot undo committed history. Committed corrections use a new case.

For an uncertain commit, retry only the same case and approved validation version.
