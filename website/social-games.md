# Join social games

[← Overview](../overview.md) · **Who:** Everyone

1. Open [Social Games](https://tzhsports.vercel.app/social-games).
2. Select available upcoming dates. Choose the session/fee option for each date and your player level.
3. Choose **Continue in WhatsApp** and check the prepared dates, times, fees and venue.
4. Edit the message if needed, then send it yourself in WhatsApp. Wait for the organiser to confirm your place.

**Expected:** The website prepares a request only. It does not reserve a place, collect payment or send the message automatically. Check the current schedule and fees on the page before contacting the organiser.
